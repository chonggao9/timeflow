========================================
TimeFlow v1.2.9 Release Notes (Google Play - versionCode: 31)
========================================

【1. 简体中文版 (zh-CN) - 适用于 Google Play Console 更新说明，严格控制在 500 字符以内】
v1.2.9 更新说明：
• 🍅 番茄时钟交互人性化：默认轻触即开始并提供 5 秒快速撤销条；支持自选「长按 1 秒防误触」开关。
• 🛡️ 弹性专注与记录保护：放弃专注支持提前结算（专注满 1 分钟即可保存，不丢工作）；收获页按返回键自动保留记录。
• ⏱️ 灵活冲刺与时长延展：专注中支持随时「+5 分钟」（上限 120 分钟）；收获页支持一键「再来 5 分钟」快速收尾。
• 🔔 到期反馈与防遗忘提醒：新增到期提醒反馈设置（铃声 / 仅轻震 / 静音）；专注暂停超过 10 分钟自动调度轻提醒。
• ⚡ 细节与启动体验升级：新增 2 分钟微启动档位；最近 3 个专注目标自动去重记忆；修复长休文案与通知权限引导。

【2. 英文版 (en-US) - For Google Play Console "What's new", within 500 characters】
v1.2.9 What's New:
• 🍅 Human-Centered Focus Timer: Tap to start instantly with a 5s undo bar; optional 1s hold-to-start anti-mistouch switch.
• 🛡️ Safe Early Finish: Early finish option saves focused time (>1m) without losing hard work; back button safely defers harvest.
• ⏱️ Flexible Duration: Add +5m on the fly (up to 120m); quick "Another 5 min" button on harvest screen for final sprints.
• 🔔 Custom Feedback: Choose between Sound, Vibrate only, or Silent; gentle reminder if paused for over 10m.
• ⚡ Polished Experience: 2-minute micro-start preset; memory chips for recent tasks; improved long-break messages.
