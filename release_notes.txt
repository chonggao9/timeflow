TimeFlow v1.2.4 Release Notes (Google Play - versionCode: 26)
========================================

【1. 简体中文版 (zh-CN) - 适用于 Google Play 更新说明，已严格控制在 500 字符以内】
v1.2.4 更新说明：
• 🍅 番茄专注交互革新：转盘手势丝滑调节时长，番茄视觉放大居中，档位下移至拇指热区，8款治愈白噪音陪伴。
• 🚀 首页架构与模式重构：分段控件丝滑切换室内专注与出行轨迹；双域数据深度隔离。
• 💾 极简免密备份系统：移除密码心智负担，秒级免密恢复；支持条数对照预览与恢复前快照一键回退。
• 🗺️ 深度洞察与详情升级：原生行程详情页、路线简图拓扑、同路线耗时智能对比、未命名地点快速补齐。
• ✨ 启动体验与细节修复：窗口与状态栏零色差对齐冷启动；沉浸式隐私协议弹窗修复；出行方式横滑胶囊条。

【2. 英文版 (en-US) - For Google Play Console "What's new", within 500 characters】
v1.2.4 What's New:
• 🍅 Focus Upgrade: Smooth circular dial gesture for timer adjustment, enlarged hero tomato, 8 soothing ambient soundscapes.
• 🚀 Redesigned Home: Refined segmented control to switch between Indoor Focus and Travel Trips; isolated data domains.
• 💾 Passwordless Fast Backup: Instant zero-delay restore with record diff preview, safety snapshot, and one-tap undo.
• 🗺️ Native Trip Insights: Native trip detail view with route schematics, same-route trip comparisons, and quick unnamed-place backfill.
• ✨ Seamless Launch & Polish: Zero-flicker launch screen matching Android window color; full-screen privacy modal fix.
